SCREENING LOGICAutomated integrity screening

Source identity, content anomalies, duplicate candidates, metadata completeness and cross-source corroboration are evaluated without presenting a screening score as proof of truth.

TRACEABILITYEvery record keeps its trail

Source URL, source identifier, publication time, ingestion time, location basis and verification state are retained where the source provides them.

MEDIAPhotos / videos when available

Media metadata is preserved from connected social or citizen sources. Missing media is shown as unavailable; no placeholder is inserted.

STORAGEPostgreSQL when configured

The production path uses centralized PostgreSQL. Local JSON fallback keeps the prototype usable when no database URL is configured.

⚠️ Data integrity & operational note

Pulse 2.0 never fabricates weather events, GPS coordinates, media, source connections or verification outcomes. Official IMD, NDMA, district administration and disaster-management instructions take precedence. Social, web and citizen signals remain review material until corroborated or manually verified.
